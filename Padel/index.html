How the tournament works

Each group plays a 4-team round robin (6 matches, 2 courts at a time). Groups alternate slot by slot: A, B, A, B, A, B. Then come the quarter-finals (slot 7), semi-finals (slot 8) the final plus the 3rd-place match between the semi-final losers (slot 9), and the 7th-place match between the two 4th-placed teams (slot 10), with two spare slots for extra time. 5th and 6th go to the two quarter-final losers, ranked by group-stage points and, if equal, by rounds won.

Teams

Type the rounds each team won. Winners are highlighted automatically, and the knockout rounds fill in once a group has played all 6 matches. A red box means the score is not valid for that match.

Scoring rules

Group matches, the 3rd-place match and the 7th-place match are best of 7 (first to 4 rounds). Quarter-finals and semi-finals are best of 9 (first to 5). The final is best of 11 (first to 6).

How the table is ranked

Group matches are best of 7 rounds. A team that wins 4 rounds wins the match and gets 3 points (loser gets 0). Ranking: points, then round difference, then total rounds won (RW). Valid scores: one team has 4–7 rounds and both teams total at most 7 (e.g. 4–0, 4–3, 5–2, 7–0).
