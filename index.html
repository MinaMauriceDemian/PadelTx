How the tournament works

Teams

Type the points each pair won; the other side fills in automatically so the match adds up. On a phone, use − and +. Every round brings new partners, and the leaderboard updates the moment a score is entered.

Enter the rounds each team won (on a phone, use − and +). Winners are highlighted automatically, and the knockout rounds fill in once a group has finished. A dashed amber box is a match still being played; a red box means the score is not possible for that match length.

Americano rules

Every round you play with a new partner against two new opponents. A match is played to a fixed total of points (set in Setup, e.g. 24): when the points add up to that total, the match is over, so 14–10 or 12–12. Every player keeps the points their pair won. If the number of players doesn’t fit the courts, players take turns sitting out and get half the match points for that round, the same as an average result. The winner is the player with the most points; ties are broken by wins, then points difference.

Scoring rules

Each match is "best of" a number of rounds: the first team to win more than half of them wins the match. Defaults: group and placement matches best of 7 (first to 4), quarter- and semi-finals best of 9 (first to 5), the final best of 11 (first to 6). The admin can change any match in the Stage column, or a whole stage under Setup → Match length.

How the table is ranked

Group matches are best of 7 rounds. Group matches are best of 7 rounds by default. The match winner gets 3 points (loser gets 0). Ranking: points, then round difference, then total rounds won (RW). A score is valid when the winner reaches more than half of the "best of" number and both teams together do not exceed it (best of 7: 4–0, 4–3, 5–2, 7–0).
